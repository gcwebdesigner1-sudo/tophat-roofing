What we do

Roofing & Exteriors

Everything above your head and around your walls, done by one crew that answers the phone.

01

Residential Roofing

Full roof replacements, new installs and inspections for homes across the valley. Kent brings real material samples to your kitchen table so you can see and feel the shingle before anything gets ordered. Most homes are torn off and re-roofed in about two days.

  • Complete tear-off and re-roof
  • 50-year architectural shingles
  • New underlayment and flashing
  • Ridge vents and attic ventilation
  • Magnet sweep of the whole yard
  • Final walk-through with the owner
02

Roof Repair & Leaks

A slow leak can rot decking for months before anyone notices. We track down where the water is actually getting in, fix it properly and tell you straight whether a repair will hold or the roof is due.

  • Leak tracing and repair
  • Missing or lifted shingles
  • Flashing and vent boots
  • Emergency tarping
  • Pre-sale roof inspections
  • Honest repair-vs-replace advice
03

Storm Damage

Wind, hail and fallen limbs hit Idaho roofs every year. We document the damage, get you dried in fast and help you through the insurance side so the claim covers what it should.

  • Wind and hail damage
  • Fallen tree damage
  • Photo documentation
  • Insurance claim help
  • Emergency dry-in
  • Full restoration
04

Commercial Roofing

Flat and low-slope systems for shops, offices, churches and multi-unit buildings. Repeat commercial customers keep calling us back because the job sites stay clean and the communication doesn't stop once the contract is signed.

  • TPO and flat roof systems
  • Low-slope replacements
  • Preventative maintenance
  • Commercial leak detection
  • Multi-building projects
  • Scheduled around your business
05

Seamless Gutters

Seamless gutters cut on site to fit your house, with downspouts placed where the water actually needs to go. Good gutters protect the fascia, the foundation and that new roof.

  • Seamless aluminum gutters
  • Downspouts and extensions
  • Gutter replacement
  • Fascia repair
  • Color matched to your trim
  • Installed with your new roof
06

James Hardie Siding

Fiber cement siding that stands up to Idaho sun, snow and wind. We install HardiePlank lap siding, panels and shingle profiles with HardieWrap underneath.

  • HardiePlank lap siding
  • Panel and shingle styles
  • HardieWrap weather barrier
  • Trim and soffit
  • Stucco repair
  • Full exterior makeovers
07

Windows & Doors

Window and door replacements that seal tight and cut down drafts. Plenty of our roofing customers have us swap a door or a few windows while the crew is already on site.

  • Window replacement
  • Energy-efficient options
  • Exterior door replacement
  • Patio doors
  • Trim and flashing
  • Clean, careful installs
08

Decks & Patio Covers

We build composite decks and patio roofs that match the house. Low maintenance, no splinters, and built to the same standard as our roofs.

  • Composite decks
  • Deck tear-out and rebuild
  • Railings
  • Patio covers
  • Patio roof replacement
  • Design help
