Our story

The owner answers the phone

Top Hat Roofing is a family business out of Kuna, Idaho. Owner Kent Shannon has spent more than 30 years in roofing, and the company runs the way he likes to work: show up when you said you would, explain everything, and leave the yard cleaner than you found it.

Customers mention the same things over and over in their reviews. Kent picks up the phone. He brings real samples so you can hold the shingle before you choose it. The crew starts early, finishes most roofs in two days, and runs a magnet over the lawn and driveway so no nails get left behind. Then Kent walks the finished roof himself.

Most of the crew has been with us a long time. That's on purpose. People who are treated well do careful work.
